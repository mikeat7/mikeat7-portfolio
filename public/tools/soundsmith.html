Soundsmith

A local speech ↔ text workshop

Turn audiobooks, podcasts, and videos into clean text — and turn text back into spoken audio. All on your own computer, nothing sent to the cloud.

100% offline GPU accelerated 30+ audio & video formats free & private

Two directions

🎤

Transcribe

Speech becomes text. Feed it an audiobook, lecture, interview, or video and get an accurate, timestamped transcript.

audio / video  →  text
🗣

Speak

Text becomes speech. Drop in a text file and get natural-sounding spoken audio out, as a WAV or MP3.

text  →  spoken audio

What makes it nice

🔒

Completely private

Runs entirely on your machine. Your files never leave your computer.

⚡

GPU powered

Uses your graphics card when available, and falls back to the CPU on its own.

📚

Handles huge files

Multi-hour audiobooks are processed in chunks — no memory meltdowns.

🔁

Stop & resume

Pause anytime. Finished items are skipped; interrupted ones pick up again.

🎬

Almost any format

Audiobooks, MP3, WAV, FLAC, MP4, MKV and more — it just works.

🧰

One-click app

A clean browser dashboard with live progress — or a simple command line.

How it works

Drop in your files

Put audio/video (to transcribe) or text (to speak) into a folder.

Press start

Pick a quality level and click go. Watch the live progress bar and log.

Collect the results

Transcripts (or spoken audio) appear in the output folder, ready to use.

Formats it understands

.aax.aaxc.m4b .mp3.m4a.aac .wav.flac.ogg .opus.wma.aiff .mp4.mkv.mov .avi.webm.wmv …and more

Your audio, your machine, your business.

Soundsmith does everything locally — no accounts, no uploads, no subscriptions. Powered by open-source engines (Whisper for listening, Piper for speaking).
